Guide

  1. Tap a part in the palette to place it on the board (switches, LEDs, +5V, GND, gates, ICs).
  2. Drag a part by its body to move it.
  3. Wire: tap an output pin (right side), then tap an input pin — or drag from one pin to another. Tap a wire to select it.
  4. Switch: tap a switch to flip it 0 ↔ 1. LEDs light up when their input is 1.
  5. ICs: connect +5V to pin 14 (VCC) and GND to pin 7, otherwise the IC stays dead — like real hardware.
  6. Delete: select a part or wire, then press Delete selected (or the Delete key).
  7. Use Experiments → Open on board to load a ready-made circuit.

Pin colours: green = logic 1, grey = logic 0. Unconnected inputs read as 0.

Signals

Power

Trainer board supply: ON

With power OFF all switches, gates and ICs output 0 and LEDs stay dark. ICs additionally need +5V on pin 14 and GND on pin 7.

Objectives

  • Verify the truth tables of basic and universal logic gates.
  • Build combinational circuits (half adder, full adder) and a simple latch.
  • Identify TTL ICs and their pin configuration.

Lab rules

  • Read the experiment before starting the virtual board.
  • Always wire VCC (pin 14) and GND (pin 7) of an IC.
  • Record every observation in your lab notebook.

Report format

  1. Experiment number, title and date
  2. Aim and apparatus
  3. Circuit diagram
  4. Truth table (observed)
  5. Discussion and conclusion

    Help

    How do I connect two pins?

    Tap the first pin (it gets a yellow ring) then tap the second pin. You can also drag between pins.

    My IC does nothing

    Connect a +5V part to pin 14 and a GND part to pin 7 of the IC, and make sure board power is ON.

    How do I remove a wire or part?

    Select it and press Delete selected.

    Does it work offline / on GitHub Pages?

    Yes. It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no backend.

    Simulation model

    Wires merge pins into nets; each net is driven by output pins (OR-ed). Gates are re-evaluated until the circuit settles, so latches hold their state.
